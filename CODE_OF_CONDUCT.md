# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Saudade is a research and engineering project maintained by DMJ Labs. We want the project to be a welcoming, constructive, and respectful environment for everyone who participates.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Give constructive technical feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, implementations, and evidence rather than individuals.
- Respect different levels of experience and backgrounds.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Credit the work of others.
- Respect applicable licenses and intellectual property rights.
- Accept reasonable correction when an error is identified.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or unwanted sexual attention.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions or contribution processes.
- Publishing private or personally identifying information without permission.
- Impersonating project maintainers or falsely claiming project endorsement.
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, data, or harmful material.
- License violations or intentional removal of required attribution.
- Conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

## Technical Disagreement

Strong technical disagreement is allowed and encouraged when it is constructive. Discussions about model architecture, datasets, benchmarks, implementation choices, and research conclusions should remain focused on the technical subject.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove contributions, restrict participation, or take other appropriate action when this Code of Conduct is violated.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions within the Saudade repository and associated project spaces when participants are representing or participating in the project.
