# Governance

## Project

Saudade is maintained by DMJ Labs.

The project focuses on small-scale language-model architecture, training, evaluation, and related engineering research.

## Maintainers

Maintainers are responsible for:

- Repository administration.
- Releases.
- Architectural direction.
- License decisions.
- Security decisions.
- Reviewing contributions.
- Maintaining project standards.

## Decision Making

Routine changes may be decided directly by maintainers.

For significant architectural or project-direction changes, maintainers may consider technical evidence, benchmarks, reproducibility, project goals, community feedback, and maintenance cost.

## Releases

Official releases are created by project maintainers.

Experimental branches, forks, and unofficial checkpoints must not be represented as official Saudade releases without maintainer approval.

## Contributions

Community contributions are governed by `CONTRIBUTING.md` and the project's `LICENSE`.
